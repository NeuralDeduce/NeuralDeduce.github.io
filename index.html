01 / THE PROJECT

A first-person survival game.
Designed for cooperative play.

NeuralDeduce is developing an original first-person survival game powered by a proprietary game engine. Exploration, resource management, and cooperative gameplay form the foundation of the game's design.

Built around a 1-4 player local co-op experience, the game aims to deliver an engaging survival adventure centered on immersion, responsiveness, and replayability. A custom-built engine allows us to bring that vision to life.
